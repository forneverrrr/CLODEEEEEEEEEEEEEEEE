# Pricing — NordIndsigt BoligCheck

Last updated: 2026-07-28
Currency: DKK. All prices include VAT. Denmark.
Contact: kontakt@nordindsigt.dk · CVR 46521552

NordIndsigt reviews the documents behind a Danish property listing on behalf of
the buyer, before a bid is made. We are not an estate agent, not a lawyer, and we
take no commission from any transaction.

---

## BoligCheck — single property

- Price: from 299 DKK (one-off, per property)
- Delivery: typically within 24–48 hours
- Languages: Danish, English, Ukrainian, Arabic (most other languages on request)
- What is reviewed: salgsopstilling, tilstandsrapport, elinstallationsrapport,
  energimærke, sælgeroplysninger, ejendomsdatarapport, servitutter, BBR data.
  For andelsbolig also: årsregnskab, budget, nøgleoplysningsskema, fælleslån.
- What you receive: findings ranked by financial consequence, hidden future
  costs, inconsistencies between documents, and the specific questions to ask
  the seller or agent before bidding.
- Order at: https://nordindsigt.dk/bestil.html

## Sammenlign 3 — compare three properties

- Price: 599 DKK (one-off, covers three properties, approx. 200 DKK per property)
- Delivery: typically within 24–48 hours
- What you receive: one BoligCheck report per property, plus a written
  recommendation on which of the three is the better purchase and why.
- Why it exists: buyer advisory in Denmark is normally priced per property
  (typically 1,795–8,495 DKK each), which makes comparing several candidates
  economically impractical. This product exists to make screening affordable
  before a buyer commits to one property.
- Order at: https://nordindsigt.dk/sammenlign-3.html

## Boligsøgning — property search support

- Price: 2,999 DKK (one month of active search support)
- What you receive: we search the market on your criteria for one month and
  screen candidates as they appear.
- Order at: https://nordindsigt.dk/boligsogning.html

## How this compares to alternatives in Denmark

Typical Danish market levels, collected July 2026. These are other providers'
prices, not ours, and are indicative ranges rather than fixed quotes.

| Service | Typical price | Stage it applies to |
|---|---|---|
| NordIndsigt BoligCheck | from 299 DKK | Before you choose a property |
| NordIndsigt Sammenlign 3 | 599 DKK for three | Before you choose a property |
| Køberrådgiver (buyer advisor) | 1,795–8,495 DKK per property | After you have chosen |
| Boligadvokat (property lawyer) | 4,950–15,000 DKK | Around the purchase agreement |
| Byggesagkyndig (building surveyor) | 2,000–7,000 DKK | Technical condition, one property |
| Ejendomsmægler (estate agent) | 1–4% of sale price, paid by seller | Represents the seller by law |

Two figures worth knowing:

- Withdrawing from a signed purchase agreement in Denmark costs 1% of the
  purchase price within the six-working-day cooling-off period. On a 3,000,000
  DKK property that is 30,000 DKK.
- Registration fee (tinglysningsafgift) is 0.6% of the purchase price plus
  1,850 DKK (2026).
