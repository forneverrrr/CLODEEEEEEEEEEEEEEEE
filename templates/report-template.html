What the listing doesn't put up front

Six signals we'd want a buyer to see first

The advert leads with “quiet,” “well-kept,” and “trygge stisystemer.” All fair. Here is what an honest Danish-reading friend would flag before the romance.

Asbestos roof, on the clock roof

Roof is corrugated eternit containing asbestos, confirmed in BBR and the condition report. Statistical service life runs to ~2056, so it isn't urgent — but eventual replacement is a special-handling cost, and ~14 plates were already changed after the Feb-2026 storm tilstandsrapport p.5–8 sælgeropl. p.19.

A safety device that doesn't fire electrical

The RCD (HFI/HPFI earth-leakage breaker) does not cut out, its test button is defective, and the board can take oversized fuses — all in the fire/shock category elrapport p.6, faults #1–3. This is cheap to misjudge and serious to ignore. Authorised electrician, before anything else.

A neighbourhood with a past — and a plan area

The adjacent Tornhøj social-housing area spent years on the state “ghettoliste” (now udsatte boligområder), and is mid-way through a large funded transformation, Kickstart Tornhøj Realdania, 2025 lex.dk. Material for resale and perception — the villa itself sits in a private pocket beside it, not inside the listed blocks.

The market here is slow liquidity

Median time-on-market for villas in 9220 is roughly 231 days DinGeo, 4 Jun 2026. This home is only at day 30 Boligsiden — fresh, but in a buyer's market. That's leverage for you and a caution for resale.

A storm left fingerprints moisture

February 2026's storm shows up across the file: damp traces in the first-floor living room, insufficient roof ventilation, and a ridge sealed with moisture-absorbing mineral wool (the wrong material) tilstandsrapport p.14–15, faults #15,16,23. A cluster worth one specialist visit.

Something has been living in the roof pests

Both skunk (eaves) spaces show insulation damaged by a marten / husmår tilstandsrapport p.14, fault #14. That means reduced insulation and a route an animal has used — ask how and whether it's been closed.

Area trajectory — the part that affects resale. Geodata — reassuring, and sourced. Radon risk: very low Boligsiden (the build year predates radon barriers, but the local level is low). Flooding: no expected impact from groundwater, sea-level, rainfall or watercourses Boligsiden / klimatilpasning.dk. Soil: the plot is in byzone and therefore “lightly contaminated” by area classification — a standard designation for Danish urban zones, not a mapped pollution site salgsopstilling p.3. 9 · Negotiation angle

Your factual leverage (analysis, not a promise)

A bid in the 2.00–2.20M zone is well-supported by the evidence, and the structure of this market favours a patient buyer. Use facts, not pressure:

  • The market is slow. ~231-day median time-on-market locally — sellers know re-listing is costly DinGeo.
  • Two same-size-class houses sold at 1.90M (Allerupvej, Ringkøbingvej), and a larger home is on the market cheaper per m² (Rebildparken 10) Boliga.
  • Priced repairs: the electrical RCD work, the roof-ventilation/ridge cluster, and wet-zone seals are concrete, document-backed line items to bring to the table tilstand/elrapport.
  • The asbestos-roof reserve is a legitimate long-term cost the price should reflect.

A discount is never guaranteed, and an over-aggressive bid can simply end the conversation. Anchor on the comp range and your specialists' quotes, and let the slow market do the rest.

12 · Limitations & confidence

Price confidence is Medium (five recent free-market comps, but a wide spread and mixed micro-locations). Condition is assessed from a full Mode-A document set, but the reports themselves are visual/spot-check surveys with their own stated reservations.
