# Long-term ownership risks + the hidden-cost iceberg

The things Danish owners hit *years after* buying — not at purchase. Each is a
"see what others miss" item. Always frame as ranges, tag `web`/`calc`/`assumption`,
and say "get binding quotes". Trigger relevance by era/heating/construction.

## A. Non-obvious structural / legal risks
- **Buried oil tank (olietank).** Pre-fjernvarme homes (esp. built ~1960–1978) may
  have an unregistered buried mazout tank. BBR registration only became mandatory in
  1970 and kommuner did not enforce it → a tank can exist and appear in *no* document.
  Leak → soil contamination. A new owner is usually **not** liable to pay for cleaning
  old contamination once the tank is out of use, but must disclose it at resale (cuts
  price), and digging it out is ~9,000–25,000 kr (+ soil tests 3,000–5,000; more if
  contaminated). **Free check:** ask the seller if the house ever had oil/печное heat
  before fjernvarme; look at the old 1976/78 drawings (often on weblager.dk, referenced
  in the tilstandsrapport) for a fyrrum/tank. Trigger: any pre-1980 home now on
  fjernvarme/varmepumpe.
- **Sewer (kloak) is legally OUTSIDE the huseftersyn.** The tilstandsrapport itself
  states a kloakinspektion is not part of the scheme. Buyers wrongly assume "3 reports
  = everything checked". Private sewer replacement is 30,000–100,000+ kr and surfaces
  nowhere automatically. Present as a **hole in the system**, not just a house risk.
- **Asbestos beyond the roof.** Hustypebeskrivelse for ~1950–1986 homes: asbestos may
  be in **tile glue, vinyl floors, pipe insulation**, eternit ducts. So a "cheap"
  re-seal of an old vinyl/tiled wet area can become asbestos handling → re-price that
  repair upward. Trigger: any flagged vinyl/tile work in a pre-1986 home.
- **Steel heating pipes cast in the concrete terrændæk.** If the energimærke says
  varmerør are steel run in the floor slab, a disclosed single leak is a **systemic
  ageing signal**, not a one-off — the next leak means breaking concrete. Trigger:
  energimærke "varmerør ... stålrør i terrændæk" + any disclosed floor-pipe leak.
- **Self-build / on-the-boundary structures.** Carport/annex built *i skel* (on the
  boundary) or self-built with unknown year + BBR mismatch → the kommune can demand
  legalisation or demolition years later, **at the current owner's cost**, and a
  boundary structure may need the neighbour's written fire-consent. Check
  byggesagsarkiv. Trigger: BBR deviations / "selvbyg" / "i skel" in the docs.
- **Hot-water exchanger / boiler age unknown** (e.g. Termix). Lifespan 15–25 yrs →
  replacement reserve. Trigger: VVS section lists it without a year.

## B. Sector-wide trends (current, 2025–2026)
- **Climate-driven insurance.** Husforsikring premiums rose up to **~27% in two years**
  (Forbrugerrådet Tænk / TV2). Insurers (e.g. Gjensidige) move to **per-address risk
  pricing**; storm/skybrud-hit homes can become hard or expensive to insure. A home
  with a documented storm event will likely see premiums rise faster than general
  inflation. Tag `web`.
- **Tax re-valuation / efterregulering 2025–26.** New ejendomsvurderinger roll out;
  if assessed higher, grundskyld/ejendomsværdiskat rise — possibly **soon after
  purchase** and retroactively (also via a grundejerforening). The salgsopstilling's
  "foreløbig"/efterregulering fine print is the local instance of this national trend.
- **Deferred maintenance surfaces in year one.** "What if the boiler breaks 3 months
  after purchase" (Bolius/Boliga) — a long-tenure seller's deferred upkeep typically
  lands on the new owner's first year.
- **General upkeep reserve.** Rule of thumb ≈ **1% of property value per year** for
  maintenance — the boring, real number nobody budgets. Tag `assumption` (rule of thumb).
- **hegnsloven.** Shared-hedge/fence costs with a neighbour are partly mandatory by
  law, not optional — plus a big plot (e.g. 900 m²) is real recurring upkeep + tool
  outlay for an ex-apartment buyer.

## C. The cost-over-time iceberg (the selling centrepiece)
Frame: *"The listing shows X kr/md. Here is the iceberg beneath it."* Build a 4-horizon
table; every figure a range tagged `assumption`/`calc`/`web`; repeat "get quotes".
- **Now (year 0):** flagged repairs from the reports (electrical safety first; roof
  ventilation; wet-zone seals; railing; sealants; own advokat). Sum ≈ realistic
  first-year outlay above price.
- **1–3 years:** degraded windows/doors; pest exclusion + re-insulation; cheap energy
  wins (loft top-up).
- **5–15 years:** appliances (replace the ~10-yr-old set in turn); eventual bathroom
  renovation; the ~1%/yr upkeep reserve.
- **20–30 years:** big-ticket — e.g. asbestos eternit roof full replacement
  (~1,000–2,000 kr/m² incl. removal/disposal); optional solceller.
- **Fine print that costs (doc-sourced where possible):** tax efterregulering;
  consumption *on top of* ejerudgift (heat + electricity + water); grundejerforening
  levies; ejerskifteforsikring beyond year 1 (and it won't cover listed defects).
- **Bottom line:** compute realistic monthly cost of ownership **ex-mortgage**
  = ejerudgift + consumption + ~1%/yr upkeep. Often ~3× the advertised ejerudgift.
  This single line is the strongest "see what others miss" moment — keep it honest.
